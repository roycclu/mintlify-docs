## FAQ Student Discount

### How do I verify my student status?

You can verify your student status by clicking the "Verify Student Status" button above. Make sure you are logged in with your university email address.

### What universities are eligible for the student discount?

We accept students from most accredited universities and colleges worldwide. If you're currently enrolled as a student, you're likely eligible! That said, we're not able to verify every university. If your school is not on the list, please notify us when prompted in the verification process.

### How much is the student discount?

Students receive 50% off on the Lovable Pro 100 credits monthly plan, or a discount of $12.50/month on all other plans.

### How long does the student discount last?

Your student discount is valid for up to 12 months. Once your discount expires you can continue using Lovable at the regular pricing. If you cancel your subscription you will lose your discount and can not reactivate it later.

### Can I transfer my discount to a friend?

No. Your student discount is only valid for the account you use to verify your status.

### Does the student discount apply to existing customers?

This discount is only valid for users on the free plan.

### I already have a Lovable account with a non-student email. Can I transfer existing projects?

The student discount is only valid on accounts with a student email. If you are the project owner you can transfer the project to another workspace you are a member of in project settings. Alternatively, you can invite your other account to join the project as an editor or admin.

### My student email will be deactivated soon. How can I maintain access to my projects?

If your student email will be deactivated soon, you can transfer the project to another workspace you are a member of in project settings. Alternatively, you can invite your other account to join the project as an editor or admin.

### I am a teacher. Can I still get the student discount?

Yes! If you are teaching at a university or school you can still get a student discount. Just make sure you are logged in with your academic email address.

### Do you offer discounts for nonprofits?

Yes. We offer a 20% discount to verified nonprofit organizations for one year. At the end of the year, you can extend the discount by resubmitting verification of your nonprofit status. To apply, please email [support@lovable.dev](mailto:support@lovable.dev) with documentation proving your organization's nonprofit status (such as 501(c)(3) certification for US organizations or equivalent for international nonprofits).

### I represent a university. Can I buy a bulk license to get a discount for my students?

You might be eligible for a substantial academic discount on our Business plan, which lets you as a teacher add and manage users sharing a central set of credits, so only one payment is needed to cover all students. Learn more about eligibility and apply through our education partners program at [lovable.dev/partners/education](https://lovable.dev/partners/education).
