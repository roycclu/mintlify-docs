## Frequently asked questions

### How do I join the Lovable community?

Two ways in: join online through our [Discord](https://discord.com/invite/lovable-dev) where 160K+ builders are active around the clock, or join IRL by attending a community event near you. Online is instant and always available. IRL events happen globally every week.

### Do I need to be technical to get involved?

Not at all. The community includes people of all skill levels, from first-time builders to experienced developers. Many of our most active members started with zero coding experience.

### What's the difference between Ambassador and Campus Leader?

Both are cohort-based programs. Ambassadors are for professionals and community builders in the broader tech ecosystem. Campus Leaders are for students who want to activate their university community. Apply to the one that fits where you are right now.

### How do I find or host a Lovable event?

Head to our [Community Events page](https://community.lovable.app/events) to browse upcoming events near you or apply to host your own.

### Can I be part of multiple community programs?

You should apply to the program that best fits you. You can always participate in the broader community (Discord, events, SheBuilds) alongside any program.

### How active is the community?

Very. Discord has 160K+ members across every timezone, events happen globally every week, and community programs run quarterly cohorts. There's always something going on.

### Do I need to speak English to participate?

Most Discord channels and resources are in English, but community events happen in local languages around the world. Many ambassadors and campus leaders run events in their native language.

### What does it cost?

Nothing. Discord is free, events are free, program applications are free, and SheBuilds buildathons are free to join.

### I have a question not listed here.

Reach out at [community@lovable.dev](mailto:community@lovable.dev) — we're happy to help.
