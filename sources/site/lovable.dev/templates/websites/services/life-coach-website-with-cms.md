## About this template

This template gives you a coaching website with a built-in content management system that looks professional and handles client bookings without expensive third-party tools. Among life coach websites, standing out means more than a nice homepage. You need a site that actually converts visitors into booked discovery calls.

The technical parts are already handled. Booking calendar with availability rules, multi-step intake forms with validation, admin dashboard for managing inquiries, program listings with drag-to-reorder. All of it done. You can focus on writing your coaching copy and filling your calendar.

The public site includes a homepage with animated hero section, an about page, a programs page with FAQ accordion, a booking page with calendar and intake flow, and a contact page with inquiry routing. The admin side gives you four management screens: bookings with status tracking and cancellation notes, contact messages with read/replied workflows, weekly availability with per-day toggles and date overrides, and a programs editor with active/inactive controls and icon selection.

Compare this to hiring a developer to build a coaching website from scratch, and you are looking at weeks of work and thousands of dollars. Compare it to a generic website builder, and you lose the booking system, the intake forms, and the admin CMS entirely. This coaching website template sits in the middle: custom enough to reflect your brand, functional enough to run your practice.

## Best Use Cases

### Discovery Call Booking Page

The booking flow is the core revenue driver for any life coach website. Visitors pick a date from your available slots, fill out a goals form with their focus area and program interest, and receive confirmation. You control availability down to the day and hour, with buffer time between sessions. No more email back-and-forth to schedule a single call.

### Program Showcase with CMS

List your coaching programs with descriptions, duration labels, and target audience details. Reorder them by dragging, toggle visibility, and update copy anytime from the admin panel. Visitors browsing life coach website examples will see a site that communicates clarity and structure, which builds trust before the first conversation.

### Client Intake and Message Management

Every booking captures name, email, phone, focus area, and goals. Every contact form submission routes to your admin inbox with inquiry type and status tracking. You get a clear picture of who is reaching out and why, without paying for a separate CRM. For coaches managing 10 to 50 inquiries a month, this replaces scattered email threads with one organized view.

### Branded Sections with Theme Controls

The life coach website design uses a warm coral and cream palette with editorial-style typography (Fraunces headings, Inter body text). Section themes like peach, blush, and ink let you create visual rhythm across pages. The style-pack system means you can swap the entire color palette and font pairing without touching individual components, so your coaching website matches your personal brand.

## Getting Started

### Step 1: Remix This Template

Open this template and remix it into your own Lovable project. You will get the full coaching site, admin CMS, booking system, and database schema ready to customize. If this template is not the right fit, you can remix a different one. No wasted work.

### Step 2: Customize Your Brand

Swap the coral palette for your brand colors by editing the style-pack CSS variables. Update the wordmark in the header, replace placeholder copy on the homepage and about page, and adjust the hero animation text to match your coaching philosophy. The sharp-edge radius is a deliberate design choice. Keep it or soften it to rounded corners with one token change.

### Step 3: Add Your Content

Sign in to the admin panel and create your coaching programs. Add names, descriptions, target audiences, and duration labels. Set your weekly availability schedule, block out vacation dates with the override tool, and configure your booking buffer hours so clients cannot book too close to an existing session.

### Step 4: Connect Your Tools

The template uses Supabase for authentication and database storage out of the box. Enable Google OAuth for admin sign-in, or stick with email and password. If you need payment collection, connect Stripe to charge for bookings. For email notifications when new bookings arrive, add a webhook or edge function to your Supabase project.

### 5\. Go Live

Publish your site with one click from Lovable. Connect a custom domain so clients find you at your own URL. Test the full booking flow on mobile to confirm the calendar, form, and confirmation all work at 375px width. Share your new site link in your email signature, social profiles, and any directories where coaches list their services.

## Features & capabilities

- Date and Time Booking

 Visitors pick an open slot from your availability calendar, with past dates and booked times automatically disabled. Buffer hours prevent back-to-back sessions.

- Multi-Step Intake Flow

 Clients submit their name, email, phone, focus area, program interest, and goals through a validated form before confirming their booking.

- Program Drag-to-Reorder

 Create, edit, and reorder coaching programs from the admin panel. Toggle active or inactive visibility and assign icons without touching code.

- Weekly Availability Schedule

 Set start and end times for each day of the week with on/off toggles. Add date overrides to block holidays or personal days with optional reasons.

- Contact Message Inbox

 Contact form submissions land in an admin table with inquiry type, status tracking, and detail panel. Mark messages as read or replied to stay organized.

- Booking Status Management

 View, search, and sort all client bookings. Open a detail panel to review intake answers, change status, or add cancellation notes.

- Section Theme Switching

 Wrap any page section in a theme class to change its background, text, and accent colors. Coral, peach, blush, and ink palettes ship ready to use.

- Style-Pack Customization

 Swap fonts, colors, border radius, and weight tokens from a single CSS file. Rebrand the entire site without editing individual components.

- Google OAuth Admin Login

 Sign in to your admin panel with Google or email and password. Authentication and row-level security protect all coach data by default.
