## Getting Started

### Step 1: Remix This Template

Click "Remix" to create your copy. You'll have a complete home and lifestyle store ready to customize—twelve products across eight collections, a full shopping cart, wishlist functionality, and a checkout flow. All the pages and features are already working—you just need to make it yours.

### Step 2: Customize Your Brand

Make the design yours. Swap the terracotta and cream palette for your brand colors, choose different fonts, and adjust layouts to match your style. The sharp, editorial design language adapts to different brand identities without losing its polish. You don't need to know code to make it look exactly how you want.

### Step 3: Add Your Content

Replace the sample products with your own inventory—upload your product photography and write descriptions that tell the story behind each piece, then add material and dimension details. Organize items into collections that make sense for your brand, with filtering and discovery tools built to highlight what matters. The product gallery features smooth image transitions, a thumbnail strip for easy navigation, and elegant controls that make every product page feel editorial. The template stays out of your way—your products stay the focus.

### Step 4: Connect Your Tools

The checkout form and cart system are structured and ready for backend connections. Lovable has built-in integrations for the tools e-commerce businesses actually use—link payment processing for real transactions, connect email marketing to capture newsletter subscribers, and add analytics to track performance as you scale.

### Step 5: Go Live

Deploy your store with one click. Lovable handles the hosting, security, and performance optimization automatically. Share your storefront link and start selling—your store goes live worldwide in minutes.

## Features & capabilities

- Parallax Hero Sections

 Scroll-responsive hero images with Ken Burns animation and content fade effects that create an immersive editorial landing experience.

- Dual-Image Product Cards

 Hover-triggered image swaps with smooth crossfade transitions show products from multiple angles without requiring clicks.

- Collection Filtering System

 URL-based filtering with horizontal scrollable category bar and five sorting options lets customers find exactly what they want.

- Rich Product Galleries

 Carousel with thumbnail strip, prev/next navigation, and crossfade transitions gives each product the presentation it deserves.

- Persistent Cart and Wishlist

 Zustand-powered state management saves customer selections between visits so they can return and complete purchases.

- Curated Typography Pairing

 Cormorant Garamond serif headings with Inter sans-serif body create a refined editorial hierarchy from hero to micro-labels.

- Scroll-Triggered Animations

 Staggered product reveals and collection card animations add polish and movement that makes browsing feel intentional.

- Sticky Product Information

 Product details and order summary panels stay visible while scrolling, keeping key information accessible during decision-making.

- Complete Checkout Flow

 Nine-field checkout form with validation ready for backend connection so you can take orders immediately after launch.

- Asymmetric Collection Grids

 12-column CSS grid layouts with varied splits create editorial magazine-style presentation that highlights featured collections.

- Animated Mobile Navigation

 Hamburger menu with staggered link animations and scroll-aware header states delivers premium mobile experience.

- Warm Scandinavian Palette

 Terracotta accents on cream backgrounds with charcoal text create a cohesive brand aesthetic that elevates artisan products.
