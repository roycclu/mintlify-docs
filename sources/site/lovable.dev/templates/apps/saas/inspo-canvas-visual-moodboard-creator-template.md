## About this template

This template gives you a visual mood board tool built around a spatial canvas. Upload images, arrange them freely, add text notes, and generate new images with AI — all on a zoomable, pannable workspace with a dot grid background.

Each board is a separate canvas where you can place items at any position and size. Drag to reposition, use corner handles to resize or rotate, and right-click for context actions like copy, paste, and delete. Everything saves automatically to the database.

The dashboard lets you create, rename, reorder, and delete boards. Each board shows a thumbnail preview and timestamp. Boards can be reordered with drag-and-drop.

Authentication is built in — users sign up with email (or Google), verify their account, and get access to their private boards. All data is scoped to the authenticated user.

## Getting Started

### Step 1: Remix This Template

Click "Remix" to create your copy. You'll have a working mood board app you can explore before adding your own content.

### Step 2: Familiarize Yourself With the Project

Go to User > Settings > Knowledge, and read what's under "Project knowledge" to understand the intended architecture and data model. You can also ask Lovable in the chat for more details.

### Step 3: Customize Your Brand

Update colors, fonts, and your app name. The design system uses semantic tokens so you can change the entire look by editing a few CSS variables.

### Step 4: Create Your First Board

Sign up, head to the dashboard, and create a new board. Upload images, add text, and arrange everything on the canvas.

### Step 5: Try AI Image Generation

Use the magic wand tool in the floating dock to generate images from text prompts directly onto your canvas.

### Step 6: Go Live

Deploy with one click and share with your team. Lovable handles hosting and performance.

### Step 7: Iterate and Make It Yours

Continue building and make any changes to the app to suit your creative workflow and preferences.

## Features & capabilities

- Infinite spatial canvas

 Place images and text anywhere on a zoomable, pannable canvas with a subtle dot grid background.

- Image upload and placement

 Upload images from your device and place them on the canvas. Drag to reposition, resize with handles, and rotate with corner controls.

- AI image generation

 Generate images from text prompts using the built-in AI tool. Created images appear directly on your canvas ready to arrange.

- Text annotations

 Add text items to your canvas for notes, labels, or captions. Double-click to edit text in place.

- Multi-board dashboard

 Create and manage multiple boards from a central dashboard. Each board shows a thumbnail preview, title, and last-updated timestamp.

- Drag-and-drop reordering

 Reorder boards on the dashboard with drag-and-drop. On the canvas, freely move and arrange all items.

- Copy and paste

 Copy selected items and paste them onto the canvas. Works with keyboard shortcuts for fast workflows.

- Undo and redo

 Full undo/redo stack for canvas actions. Never worry about losing work from an accidental move or delete.

- Context menu

 Right-click any item for quick actions — copy, paste, delete, and more.

- Floating toolbar

 A dock at the bottom of the canvas gives you quick access to tools — select, upload, text, delete, download, and AI generate.

- Keyboard shortcuts

 Power-user shortcuts for select, copy, paste, undo, delete, zoom, and pan. A built-in shortcuts panel shows all available keys.

- Board export

 Download your canvas as an image to share or use in presentations.

- Authentication

 Email-based signup and login with Google sign-in support. Users verify their email before accessing the app.

- Responsive design

 Works on desktop and adapts to different screen sizes with a responsive canvas and UI.

- Dark and light mode

 Toggle between dark and light themes from the dashboard navbar.
