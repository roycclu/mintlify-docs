# **Code of conduct**

## **Our commitment**

At Lovable, we're not just building apps and websites — we're building a community. And communities are made of people. We want everyone here to feel welcome, respected, and safe. Whether you're creating, collaborating, or just exploring what's possible, you're part of something bigger. We're committed to making this space supportive and inclusive, because the best communities are the ones where everyone belongs.

What makes this community work is simple: people showing up and treating each other well. When problems happen, we address them with care and transparency.

## **Our values**

Our values aren't just words on a page, they're how we decide to show up. When you share a fresh idea, you open up the space to collaborate and grow together. When you offer thoughtful feedback, it raises the bar for what we all create. When you help someone build their first project, you're showing them what's actually possible.

We're building a community where everyone leaves things a little better than they found them. That means being generous with your knowledge, curious about different approaches, and willing to learn from people whose experiences don't mirror your own.

It's not about being perfect. It's about showing up with intention, showing up as yourself, staying open to possibility, and remembering that the person on the other side of the screen is human too.

When we all lean into these values, something remarkable happens. We don't just build better software. We build a better way of building.

## **How to show up**

Whether you're joining us online or at an event IRL, the way you show up matters. We're a global community. We come from different places, speak different languages, and carry different stories. We're here because we believe ideas shouldn't stay stuck in our heads, but get built. Keeping this space open for everyone is how we make sure they do.

Here's what this looks like in practice:

**Communicate thoughtfully.** Be clear, be kind, and assume good intent. Give feedback with care and respect. Make room for new perspectives because new ideas come from unexpected places.

**Stay open.** You'll meet people who build differently, think differently, and solve problems in ways you haven't seen before. Lead with curiosity, and listen to understand.

**Build each other up.** Share what you know. Celebrate wins that aren't yours. Because when you help someone bring their idea to life, you add momentum to the whole community.

## **Communication**

Our communities work best when everyone helps keep them focused and useful. A few practical things to keep in mind:

- Treat others with respect. Do not use offensive or hateful language or conduct that targets identity, background, physical appearance, personal circumstances, nationality, ethnicity, age, gender, beliefs, or life choices.
- Be honest. No misleading or fraudulent behavior.
- Respect privacy. Don't share someone's personal information or take photos without consent.
- Be inclusive. Recognize diverse perspectives and experiences. Encourage constructive dialogue and participation of all community members.
- Stay helpful. No spam, unsolicited promos, or referral links.

Breaching these guidelines could mean removing content, suspending access, or removing you from Lovable spaces. We take these decisions seriously and handle them on a case-by-case basis.

This code applies to all Lovable Community initiatives such as events, programs, and online community channels across different platforms. Learn more [here](https://lovable.dev/community).

By being here, you're agreeing to these guidelines, our [Platform Rules](https://lovable.dev/platform-rules) and our [Terms of Use](https://lovable.dev/terms).

## **Reporting**

If you experience or witness a violation of this code, please let us know immediately. You can contact us directly at [community@lovable.dev](mailto:community@lovable.dev).

## **You're part of something bigger**

Every person in our community adds to what Lovable becomes. The ideas shared, the support given, and the things built. Together, this community is creating products, solutions, and innovations that will reach real people and change how they live and work.

We're glad you're here.
