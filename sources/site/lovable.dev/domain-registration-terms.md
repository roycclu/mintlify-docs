It is your responsibility to keep track of your domain name expiration dates and to ensure timely renewal. The specific processes and timelines for domain expiration, reactivation, redemption, and deletion are governed by the Name.com Registration Agreement, applicable ICANN policies (including the Expired Registration Recovery Policy), and the policies of the applicable registry operator.
