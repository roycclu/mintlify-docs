# How can we help?

Choose the form that matches your situation. Picking the right inbox helps us review and act on it properly. Our team reviews every submission carefully and will follow up by email.

Please note that incomplete submissions, or submissions sent to an inbox unrelated to your report type, will not be reviewed or actioned. We appreciate your cooperation in helping us handle every report.

- [Abuse Report\\ \\ Report a Lovable-hosted project that violates our policies.](https://lovable.dev/abuse/report)
- [Website Takedown Appeal\\ \\ Submit an appeal if your project was taken down and you believe the decision was a mistake.](https://lovable.dev/abuse/project-appeal)
- [Disabled Account Appeal\\ \\ Submit an appeal if your Lovable account was disabled and you believe the decision was a mistake.](https://lovable.dev/abuse/account-appeal)
- [DMCA Takedown Notice\\ \\ File a copyright infringement notice with Lovable's designated copyright agent.](https://lovable.dev/abuse/dmca)
- [DMCA Counter-Notification\\ \\ Dispute a DMCA takedown if you believe your project was removed by mistake.](https://lovable.dev/abuse/dmca-counter)
- [Trademark Complaint\\ \\ Report content on Lovable that you believe infringes your trademark.](https://lovable.dev/abuse/trademark)

## Our Commitment

### Security First

Your security and privacy are our top priorities. All reports are handled confidentially.

### Responsible Disclosure

We follow responsible disclosure practices and appreciate your help in keeping Lovable secure.

### Zero Tolerance

We maintain a zero-tolerance policy for abuse and take immediate action when verified.
