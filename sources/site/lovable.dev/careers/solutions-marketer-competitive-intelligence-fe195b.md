# Solutions Marketer, Competitive Intelligence

**TL;DR -** Lovable is looking for a Solutions Marketer focused on Competitive Intelligence to own how we track, understand, and win against the competition — building the research, field-facing content, and insights that sharpen everything from live deals to product roadmap to company narrative. This role shapes how Lovable shows up in every competitive conversation, at one of the fastest-growing companies in Europe.

## Why Lovable?

Lovable is the software creation platform that gives people the power to act on the problems closest to them. For decades, turning an idea into software required so much capital, technical fluency, and time that many ideas never came to life. Lovable is the counterargument: a platform for all people with ideas, ambition, and problems worth solving. From solopreneurs to small business owners to teams at companies like Adidas and Zendesk, people have built over 60 million projects on Lovable since its launch in November 2024. And we’re just getting started.

We’re building a generational company from Stockholm, with growing teams in London, Boston, New York, and San Francisco. Our team is small, talent-dense, and moving quickly, with a culture rooted in extreme ownership, high velocity, and low-ego collaboration. We look for people who care deeply, ship fast, and are eager to make a dent in the world.

Lovable is one of TIME’s 100 Most Influential Companies and has been recognized on the Forbes AI 50 and CNBC Disruptor 50, reflecting our momentum as one of Europe’s fastest-growing AI companies and one of the most ambitious places to build in this next era of software.

## What we’re looking for

As a Solutions Marketer focused on Competitive Intelligence, you'll own how Lovable understands and wins against the competition. You'll build the function from the ground up: the research, the battlecards and talk tracks the field relies on, and the operating rhythm that keeps the whole company sharp on a market that changes weekly. 

Competitive intelligence is the core of the role; the rest flexes across solutions marketing — the sales assets, narratives, and field content that help our enterprise teams win. We're looking for someone who comes in with a strong point of view on the AI landscape, earns trust with the field quickly, and drives outcomes without needing a team behind them.

You'll thrive in this role if you have:

- 8+ years of experience in competitive intelligence, market intelligence, product marketing, solutions engineering, or a related field at a B2B SaaS, developer tools, or AI company.

- Followed the AI and software creation landscape closely enough to have a clear point of view on where it's heading.

- Produced competitive and sales-facing content that teams used in real deals, on real deadlines.

- Turned competitive analysis into a recommendation that leadership acted on.

- A high bar for accuracy. Every claim you publish is sourced, dated, and verified, and you retire talking points that no longer hold up.

- Written for both technical and executive audiences without losing precision.

## What you’ll do

- Own the competitive landscape: track competitors across AI platforms, developer tools, and software creation, and maintain the company's view of where we win and where we're vulnerable.

- Build and maintain the battlecards, talk tracks, and customer-facing comparisons that sales and customer success rely on, and keep them current as the market moves.

- Respond quickly when competitors launch: assess the implications, update our positioning, and get guidance to the field.

- Design and run the competitive operating model: monitoring and alerts, research cadence, refresh cycles, and a single source of truth the company trusts.

- Flex across solutions marketing: build the sales assets, pitch narratives, and persona content the field needs beyond competitive.

- Set the standard for competitive claims: everything we say externally is sourced, verified, and customer-safe.
