## Frequently asked questions

### Can Lovable connect to our HRIS?

Yes, Lovable can connect to HRIS systems through APIs and webhooks. You can sync employee data, trigger workflows based on HRIS events, and keep information current without manual updates.

### Do I need engineering to build HR workflows?

No, you do not need engineering. People teams can build and publish onboarding flows and resource hubs independently, involving engineers only for deep system integrations.

### Can I build employee onboarding flows with Lovable?

Yes, you can build onboarding flows that include role selection, task sequencing, and progress tracking without third-party onboarding platforms.

### How do we control who can see and edit employee information?

You can set role-based permissions to control access. Define who can view, edit, or publish employee resources, and restrict sensitive information to specific teams or individuals.

### Can Lovable replace our intranet or employee portal?

Lovable can unify HR resources, guides, and workflows into a central internal page, functioning as a lightweight employee portal without replacing core intranet systems.

### Is Lovable secure for enterprise teams?

Yes, Lovable supports enterprise-grade controls such as role-based access and governed publishing workflows. Teams can limit who can edit, review, and publish.
