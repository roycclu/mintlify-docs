Anyone can build internal tools on Lovable, no engineering support required. This includes sales, marketing, product, ops, people, and finance teams.

Yes, Lovable can connect with your existing systems either through native connectors or custom connectors. Learn more about our connectors [here](https://lovable.dev/connect).

### Are internal tools production ready?

Yes! Lovable internal tools are production ready. We have automatic security scans, SSO, audit logs, and more out of the box to make sure your app meets your security and compliance requirements.

### How do I update my internal tool?

Updates and changes to your Lovable internal tools are conversational, just like every other app. When your processes evolve, you can chat with Lovable to make changes.
