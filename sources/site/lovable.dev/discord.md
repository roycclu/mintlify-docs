## Frequently asked questions

### What is the Lovable Discord and who is it for?

The Lovable Discord is the online home of the Lovable community. It's a space for builders and creators of all skill levels to connect, share their work, and grow together. Whether you're just getting started or building for clients, there's a place for you here.

### Do I need a paid Lovable account to join?

Nope. The Discord is open to everyone. You don't need a Lovable account at all to join and start getting involved.

### Do I need to be technical?

Not at all. The community includes complete beginners and experienced builders alike. What matters most is curiosity and a willingness to help others learn and build.

### Can I get help with my specific project?

Yes. The community and Champions are happy to dig into project-specific questions, whether that's a build challenge, an integration, or something that's just not working the way you expected. Note: Discord is not an official support platform. All assistance comes from fellow builders. For official support, visit [Lovable support](https://lovable.dev/support).

### What's a Discord "server"?

A Discord server is like a Slack workspace — a shared space with channels for different topics and purposes. The Lovable server has channels for support, showcasing builds, announcements, and more.

### How do I get started?

Create a Discord account at discord.com if you don't have one, then join the Lovable server at [https://discord.gg/lovable-dev](https://discord.com/invite/lovable-dev). Once you're in, an onboarding flow will help you find your way around.

### How do I become a Champion?

Champions are recognized members who've shown leadership, helpfulness, and a genuine commitment to the community over time. Keep showing up, keep helping others, and keep building. The path tends to find people who are already living it.
