Why connectors

## Your tools, your workflow

- ### Your app doesn’t clock out when you do

 - Connectors work offline, so scheduled tasks and background jobs keep running without prompting.
 - Lovable monitors your connections and flags anything that needs fixing, straight to your inbox.
- ### Build with context, not just instructions

 - Connect once, and Lovable speaks to your stack while you build.
 - You explain less. Lovable reaches for the right tool at the right time.
- ### Let your users connect their own tools

 - Each person connects their own accounts at sign-in, including their Drive, calendar, and CRM.
 - Credentials never touch your app. Lovable’s gateway handles OAuth, token refresh, and revocation.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do connectors work in Lovable?

Connectors let Lovable read and write data from your favorite tools. Once you enable a connector, Lovable can pull in live data, trigger actions, and build features that stay in sync with your existing stack.

### Which connectors are available?

We support tools like Linear, Notion, Stripe, Slack, Shopify, Google Workspace, and more. New connectors are added regularly — check the full list on this page.

### How do I add a connector to my project?

From your Lovable project, open the integrations panel and select the connector you want. Follow the setup steps and Lovable will be ready to use your data.

### Can I connect a tool that isn’t listed?

Yes. If a tool has an API and supports MCP (Model Context Protocol), you can connect it. Just select “Connect it yourself” to get started.

### Do connectors use my Lovable credits?

Connectors themselves don’t cost credits. Credits are only used when Lovable generates or modifies code. Connecting to your tools is free.
