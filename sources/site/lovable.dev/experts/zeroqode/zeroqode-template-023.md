## Lead CRM

A lightweight lead management platform to capture, qualify, and organize inbound leads — built for teams with multiple acquisition channels.

Loved by people who build SaaS

Created by[Z![](https://storage.googleapis.com/lovable-assets/partners/zeroqode/logo.png)Zeroqode](https://lovable.dev/experts/zeroqode)

ReactLovable

What's Included

Landing Page & Lead Forms — Public landing with customizable forms for inbound lead capture

Chat Inbox — Communicate with website visitors and leads directly from the app

Leads Dashboard — Centralized view of all inbound leads and their current status

Lead Management — View, edit, assign, and qualify leads

Campaign Management — Create campaigns, define budgets, and track lead sources

Email & Social Campaign Context — Attribute leads to email or social media campaigns

Task Management — Create and assign follow-up tasks linked to leads

Manual Lead Entry & Import — Add leads manually or via bulk import

Basic Reports & Activity Overview — High-level visibility into leads and campaigns

User Access & Settings — Manage users, assignments, and configuration
