## FinanceFlow

FinanceFlow dashboard is an analytics template for tracking revenue, expenses, cash flow, and KPIs in one place

Loved by people who help others make decisions with data

Created by[Z![](https://storage.googleapis.com/lovable-assets/partners/zeroqode/logo.png)Zeroqode](https://lovable.dev/experts/zeroqode)

ReactLovable

What's Included

Executive Overview — MRR/Revenue, Expenses, Gross Margin, Net Profit, Cash balance.

Cash Flow — inflows/outflows by month, burn rate, runway estimator.

Revenue Analytics — MRR/ARR, new vs. expansion vs. churn, top products.

Expense Analytics — Opex by category (Payroll, Marketing, Tools), variance vs. budget.

AR / AP Tracker — invoices due, aging buckets, expected payments.

Transactions Table — searchable ledger with tags, categories, and notes.

Import & Mapping — CSV import for bank/Stripe exports, column mapping, dedupe.

Filters & Saved Views — date range, department, product, region; save custom views.

Responsive UI — optimized charts and tables for desktop and mobile.
