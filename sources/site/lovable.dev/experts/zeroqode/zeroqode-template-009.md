What's Included

Home — quick start panel for resume or cover letter.

Resume Builder — structured editor with AI-suggested bullet points per experience.

Cover Letter Generator — dynamic prompt box for tone, length, and role specifics.

Job Post Analyzer — paste or upload job description; AI extracts key requirements.

Document Manager — save, edit, and export documents as PDF or Word.

Templates Gallery — pre-built layouts for professional, creative, or modern styles.

Responsive Layout — optimized for desktop, tablet, and mobile use.
