## Video Editor

Video Editor is a professional video editing and collaboration platform — upload, edit, manage, and export videos with precision and team control, all in one modern workspace.

Loved by people who build SaaS

What's Included

Dashboard / Overview — total projects, videos, users, storage stats, and export queue overview.

Editor — trim, crop, preview, format (aspect ratio, resolution, quality), and export settings.

Projects & Reports — list of all user and admin projects, export history, and usage reports.

User Modes — Standard User and Admin Mode with separate dashboards and permissions.

Uploads — drag-and-drop upload with live progress and playback.

Exports Management — track pending/processing/completed exports, view results, and retry failed ones.

Collaboration & Team Roles — manage editors, assign roles (Admin, User, Viewer), and review activity logs.

Settings & Branding — customize brand, manage storage, export presets, and fine-tune team environment.
