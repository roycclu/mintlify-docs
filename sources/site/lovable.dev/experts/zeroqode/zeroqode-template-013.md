## SubtitleAI

AI-powered video subtitle editor- upload your video, auto-generate subtitles, edit them inline, and export in SRT or burned-in format in seconds.

What's Included

Home / Upload — drag & drop or browse to upload video files (MP4, MOV, WEBM).

AI Transcription — automatic speech-to-text with timestamp alignment.

Subtitle Editor — inline text editing with pause & preview sync.

Timeline View — scrub timeline, adjust subtitle durations visually.

Translation Mode — optional AI translation into selected languages.

Preview Player — video playback with live subtitle overlay.

Export Options — download .srt, .vtt, or export burned-in subtitles to video.

Font & Style Controls — customize subtitle font, size, color, and position.

Multi-language Projects — manage and export multiple language tracks.

Responsive Layout — fully optimized for desktop and tablet editing.
