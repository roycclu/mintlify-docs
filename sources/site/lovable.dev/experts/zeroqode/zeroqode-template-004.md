## Signature Crafter

Signature Crafter lets you design branded email signatures and create QR codes in seconds

Loved by people who build SaaS

Created by[Z![](https://storage.googleapis.com/lovable-assets/partners/zeroqode/logo.png)Zeroqode](https://lovable.dev/experts/zeroqode)

ReactLovable

What's Included

Home / Signature Builder — form fields with live preview (name, title, company, phone, email, website)

Template Library — preset layouts (left/right avatar, stacked, minimal) with spacing options.

Brand Kit — logo/avatar upload, brand colors, font selection, icon style (filled/outline)

Social Links — add/remove socials (LinkedIn, X, Instagram, YouTube, etc.) with reorder & visibility toggles.

CTA & Legal — optional call-to-action line and disclaimer/footer text.

Dark/Light Variants — preview and adjust contrast for dark-mode email clients.

QR Code Generator — create QR from URL.

Placement & Download — attach QR to the signature or export as standalone PNG/SVG/PDF for print.

Export & Install Guides — copy HTML for Gmail/Outlook/Apple Mail + asset pack download.
