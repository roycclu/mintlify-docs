## Content Studio

A fully featured AI-powered content creation workspace—generate images, write posts, manage assets, and build your brand kit in one place

Loved by people who build SaaS

What's Included

Home Dashboard – personalized greeting, recent assets, recent posts, quick creation shortcuts, and AI generation entry points.

Asset Library – unified gallery of all images & videos: filters, sorting by date, “AI generated only” toggle, upload new asset, generate with AI modal.

AI Image Generator – modal for image generation with prompt input, aspect ratio selector, style presets, and real-time output.

Posts – section to create & manage social media posts with caption editor, AI caption generator, multi-platform preview.

New Post Editor – caption editor, hashtag assistant, AI suggestions, platform/device/theme preview, export tools.

Brand Kit – upload brand assets, define colors, logos, fonts (centralized for consistent output).

Navigation Sidebar – persistent workspace navigation with icons, section highlighting, and user account access.

Responsive Layout – optimized for desktop, tablet, and mobile.
