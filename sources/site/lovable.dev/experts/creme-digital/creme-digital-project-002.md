# Survival Sports

Survival Sports will be a sports betting app where users can purchase tickets to build out their projected winners for the NFL season, with the payout being split among the “survivors” at the end of the season. The platform supports both desktop and mobile web, with in-app and email notifications, an admin dashboard for schedule and user management, and a CMS-powered blog for sports content.
