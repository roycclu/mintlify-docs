## Frequently Asked Questions

### What can I add to my app with Lovable Cloud?

Lovable Cloud gives your app a full backend with no setup - a database, user authentication, file storage, and serverless functions. Just describe what you need and Lovable builds it for you.

### Can I connect other tools and integrations?

Yes. Lovable ships with 50+ native connectors across databases, analytics, email, content, and productivity tools, with hundreds more available through built-in automation. Most connect seamlessly in a few clicks, so you can add the services your app needs without manual setup.

### Can I accept payments in my app?

Yes. Lovable has built-in payments, so you can add subscriptions and one-time checkout to any app. Set up a test checkout in minutes and go live the same day, with your keys stored securely and used only where they are needed.

### How do credits work?

Credits are used when you send a message to Lovable, not while you're typing or editing. How many depends on the complexity of the request. You can check your remaining credits and usage anytime in your workspace settings.

### How do I get help with my project?

Start with the search above and the docs - most questions are answered there instantly. For community help, join our Discord. If you're on a paid plan and need help with your account, billing, or a platform issue, use the support form on this page.
