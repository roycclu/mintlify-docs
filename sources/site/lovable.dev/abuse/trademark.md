# Trademark Complaint

Use this form to report content on Lovable that you believe infringes your trademark. We can only review and act on a complete report. We may request further information before completing our review.

Reporting the copying of content — text, images, code, or design — rather than use of your trademark? That is a copyright matter. Please use the [Copyright (DMCA) Complaint form](https://lovable.dev/abuse/dmca) instead.

If you submit this complaint, we will provide the rights owner's name, your email address, and the nature of your report to the project owner you are reporting. They may use this to contact you about the report and attempt to resolve the issue. For this reason, you may wish to provide a valid business or professional email address.
