# Disabled Account Appeal

Submit an appeal if your Lovable account was disabled and you believe the decision was a mistake.

Thank you for contacting Lovable Trust & Safety and for taking the time to submit your appeal. We take all appeals seriously and will review your case as thoroughly as possible.

To help us process your appeal efficiently, please complete the form below. Incomplete submissions, or submissions sent to an inbox unrelated to your report type, will not be processed.
