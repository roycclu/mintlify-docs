## Frequently asked questions

### Can I keep everything on brand?

Yes, you can keep everything on brand by using shared components, styles, and reusable sections across pages, in addition to our design systems feature. Teams can standardize core layouts while still making campaign-specific edits.

### Do I need engineering?

No, you do not need engineering. Sales teams can draft, iterate, and publish digital experiences independently, and involve engineering only for deeper integrations or advanced workflows.

### What integrations work with Lovable?

Lovable works with common integrations for analytics, tracking, and lead capture. Teams connect it to their CRM and analytics stack depending on how they measure performance and route form submissions. Learn more about our integrations [here](https://lovable.dev/connect).

### Does Lovable support SEO?

Yes — and it goes well beyond the basics. Apps built after May 2026 ship with server-side rendering (SSR) so search engines and AI tools like ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity can read and index your content immediately. Apps built earlier get pre-rendering automatically, no action required. Lovable also has Semrush built in, so you can ask how your app is performing in search, find keyword opportunities, and act on them without leaving the chat. You can also run an SEO review on demand to check metadata, sitemap, alt text, canonical tags, and more — and fix issues in one click.

### Can Lovable replace our CMS for landing pages?

Yes, Lovable can replace a CMS for many landing-page workflows, especially for campaigns that require frequent iteration. Some teams still keep a CMS for blogs or long-form publishing.

### How does publishing work?

Publishing works through a draft-to-live workflow so teams can review changes before they go live. You can also restrict who has permission to publish.

### Is Lovable secure for enterprise teams?

Yes, Lovable supports enterprise-grade controls such as role-based access and governed publishing workflows. Teams can limit who can edit, review, and publish.
