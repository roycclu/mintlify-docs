## Get up and running

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up at [lovable.dev](https://lovable.dev/signup). The free plan gives you 5 build credits per day with no credit card required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe your idea">
    Type what you want to build in the chat. Drop in screenshots, paste a Notion doc, or just describe it in plain language — Lovable takes it from there.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refine and iterate">
    Use the live preview to see your app take shape. Ask for changes in plain language, tweak designs visually, or switch to Code Mode for direct edits.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish your app">
    Hit **Publish** to deploy your app instantly. Connect a custom domain, enable authentication, and add payments when you're ready to go live.
  </Step>
</Steps>
