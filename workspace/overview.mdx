Your workspace is your home in Lovable — the organization-level container that holds all your projects, team members, billing, and configuration. Think of it like a company account: every project you build lives inside a workspace, and everyone you invite shares its resources. Whether you're a solo builder or running a team of dozens, understanding the workspace structure helps you stay organized, control costs, and set standards that carry across every app you ship.

## Workspace vs. Project

A **workspace** is the top-level organizational unit. It contains:

- All projects your team creates
- Members and their roles
- Billing and credit balance
- Workspace-level settings (design systems, knowledge, connectors)

A **project** is an individual app or website. Each project has its own codebase, deployment, and configuration, but it inherits workspace-level design systems, knowledge, and connector access set by admins.

<Info>
  Every Lovable plan belongs to a workspace. Credits are shared across all members of the workspace, not allocated per person.
</Info>

## Creating and Switching Workspaces

Lovable creates your first workspace automatically when you sign up. If you need a separate environment — for a different team, client, or business unit — you can create additional workspaces from your account menu.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your account menu">
    Click your workspace name in the top-left corner of the Lovable dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Create new workspace">
    Choose **Create new workspace** from the dropdown. Give it a name that reflects its purpose (e.g., your company name, client name, or team).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure workspace settings">
    Add a logo, set notification preferences, and invite your first members. You can update these settings any time under **Workspace Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch between workspaces">
    Use the same workspace switcher in the top-left to jump between workspaces at any time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If you're an agency or consultant managing multiple clients, create a separate workspace per client. Each workspace has isolated projects, members, billing, and settings.
</Tip>

## Managing Projects

Projects live on your workspace dashboard. From there you can create, rename, archive, and delete projects without leaving the view.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Create">
    Click **New project** on your workspace dashboard, then describe what you want to build. Lovable starts the project and drops you straight into the editor.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Rename">
    Open the project's three-dot menu on the dashboard and select **Rename**. The new name appears everywhere — in your dashboard, the URL slug, and GitHub if you've synced.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Archive">
    Archive projects you're not actively working on. Archived projects don't count against any limits but remain accessible if you need them later. Select **Archive** from the project menu.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Delete">
    Deleting a project is permanent and removes the deployment. Select **Delete** from the project menu. If you've synced to GitHub, the repository is not deleted — your code stays safe.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Credit Management

Credits are the currency Lovable uses to measure usage — building, hosting, and AI features all draw from a shared workspace credit balance.

- **Shared pool**: every member of the workspace builds from the same credit balance.
- **Monthly credit limits**: workspace owners and admins can set a default monthly credit limit for all members, plus per-member overrides, so no one person can exhaust the team's budget.
- **Top-ups**: purchase additional credits at any time from **Workspace Settings → Billing**.
- **Credit visibility**: admins can see credit usage broken down by member and project.

<Note>
  Plans are priced by the credits they include, not by the number of seats. Inviting more team members does not change your subscription cost — only total credit usage does.
</Note>

To set per-member credit limits:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Workspace Settings → Members">
    Find the member whose limit you want to adjust.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit the credit limit">
    Click the edit icon next to their monthly credit allocation and enter the limit in credits. Leave it blank to apply the workspace default.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a workspace default">
    In **Workspace Settings → Billing**, set a default monthly credit limit applied automatically to new members.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Workspace Settings

Access **Workspace Settings** from the sidebar or the workspace menu to configure:

| Setting | Description |
|---|---|
| **Name & logo** | Display name and logo shown to all members |
| **Notifications** | Configure email and in-app notification preferences |
| **Billing** | Manage your subscription plan, payment method, credit top-ups, and invoices |
| **Members** | Invite members, assign roles, and set credit limits |
| **Security** | Configure SSO, SCIM, and security scan policies |
| **Connectors** | Connect shared integrations (Supabase, Stripe, Slack, etc.) available across all projects |

## Design Systems

Design Systems let you define workspace-level brand standards — colors, typography, component styles — that apply to all new projects your team creates.

When you configure a Design System for your workspace, Lovable applies your brand palette, font choices, and component defaults whenever a new project is started. This keeps every app your team ships visually consistent without requiring each builder to configure styles from scratch.

To set up your workspace Design System:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Workspace Settings → Design System">
    Navigate to the Design System tab in your workspace settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Define your brand tokens">
    Set your primary and secondary colors, font families, border radius preferences, and any other global tokens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and apply">
    Save your design system. All new projects will inherit these defaults automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  You can override workspace design system settings at the project level without affecting other projects. Use the project-level Design mode for one-off customizations.
</Tip>

## Project Knowledge

Project Knowledge lets you set workspace-wide context — your brand voice, API patterns, coding standards, naming conventions — so Lovable automatically follows them on every build, in every project.

Unlike design systems, which govern visual output, Knowledge governs how Lovable reasons about your organization: what your product does, which libraries you prefer, how you structure data, and what conventions to follow.

Set workspace Knowledge in **Workspace Settings → Knowledge**, then optionally add project-specific context at the project level to layer more detail on top.
