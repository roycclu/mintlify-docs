## Direct Support

Email support is available for paying users — Pro, Business, and Enterprise plans — for account, billing, and platform issues.

**Contact:** [support@lovable.dev](mailto:support@lovable.dev)

<Note>For the fastest resolution, include your project URL and the exact error message or behavior in your support request. Vague reports take longer to diagnose and resolve.</Note>

### What to Include in Your Request

Providing complete context up front means the support team can act immediately rather than asking follow-up questions.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Required information">
    Every support request should include:

    - **Account email** — the email address associated with your Lovable account
    - **Project URL** — the URL of the project in the Lovable editor (e.g. `lovable.dev/projects/abc123`)
    - **Description of the issue** — what you expected to happen and what actually happened
    - **Steps to reproduce** — the exact sequence of actions that triggers the problem
    - **Error messages** — copy the full text of any error, including stack traces from the browser console
    - **Browser and operating system** — e.g. "Chrome 124 on macOS Sonoma"
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Helpful extras">
    These aren't required but can speed up resolution significantly:

    - A **screenshot or screen recording** showing the problem
    - The **message history** in your project if the issue involves a specific Lovable output
    - Any **recent changes** you made before the issue started — "it worked yesterday before I added Stripe"
    - The **network tab** output from browser DevTools if it's an API or data issue
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Enterprise Support

Enterprise customers receive a dedicated support experience designed for teams with production workloads and compliance requirements.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Priority SLAs">
    Enterprise plans include guaranteed response time SLAs so you always know when to expect a resolution. SLA terms are defined in your Enterprise agreement.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="White-glove onboarding">
    Your team gets hands-on workspace configuration support during rollout — including SSO/SAML setup, SCIM provisioning, role configuration, and workspace policy review. You won't be left to figure it out from documentation alone.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Security and compliance review">
    Enterprise support includes assistance with security documentation, data protection agreement (DPA) review, and compliance evidence for SOC 2, ISO 27001, and GDPR requirements. See the [Trust Center](https://trust.lovable.dev) for current certifications and documentation.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Direct product team access">
    Enterprise customers get direct access to Lovable's product and engineering team for roadmap input, custom integration planning, and escalation of critical issues.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Dedicated expert guidance">
    Your account includes an expert point of contact who knows your workspace configuration, understands your use case, and can provide proactive guidance as your team scales.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

To discuss Enterprise plans, [contact the team](https://lovable.dev/for-work) or reach out to [support@lovable.dev](mailto:support@lovable.dev).

## Community Resources

The Lovable community is one of the most active resources available to you. Whether you're stuck on a specific problem or looking for inspiration, there's always someone who's been there.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Discord — real-time community help">
    Join at [discord.com/invite/lovable-dev](https://discord.com/invite/lovable-dev). The community includes channels for general help, showcasing projects, sharing prompts, and discussing specific integrations like Supabase and Stripe. It's the fastest way to get a second opinion on a problem.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Blog and guides">
    The [Lovable blog](https://lovable.dev/blog) covers tutorials, product updates, builder stories, and deep dives into specific use cases. Useful when you're looking for patterns and best practices beyond the docs.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Video tutorials">
    Step-by-step video walkthroughs are linked from the [documentation sitemap](https://lovable.dev/sitemap/docs). These are especially helpful for visual learners or when written instructions aren't enough to understand a flow.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Templates gallery">
    The templates gallery at [lovable.dev/templates](https://lovable.dev/templates) provides pre-built starting points for common app types — SaaS, e-commerce, portfolios, internal tools, and more. Starting from a template is faster than building from scratch and gives you a solid reference for structure and patterns.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Reporting Issues

Use these dedicated channels to report sensitive or policy-related issues:

| Type | Link |
|------|------|
| **Security vulnerability** | [lovable.dev/security-issues](https://lovable.dev/security-issues) — responsible disclosure program |
| **Abuse** | [lovable.dev/abuse](https://lovable.dev/abuse) — report misuse of the Lovable platform |
| **DMCA / copyright** | [lovable.dev/dmca](https://lovable.dev/dmca) — copyright takedown requests |

<Warning>Do not email security vulnerabilities to the general support address. Use the responsible disclosure form at lovable.dev/security-issues to ensure your report is handled by the security team with appropriate confidentiality.</Warning>
