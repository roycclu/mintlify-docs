Publishing in Lovable is as simple as clicking a button. When you're ready to share your work with the world, hit **Publish** in the top-right corner of the editor and Lovable instantly deploys the current state of your app to a publicly accessible `*.lovable.app` URL — no CI/CD pipelines, no server configuration, no deployment scripts required. Every publish automatically triggers a security scan, so your users are always protected.

## How Publishing Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Build and edit your app">
    Make changes in the Lovable editor — chat with the AI, tweak UI visually, or edit code directly. Your work-in-progress stays in the editor and does **not** affect your live app until you explicitly publish.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Publish">
    When you're happy with your changes, click the **Publish** button in the top-right corner of the editor. Lovable begins deploying your current app state immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Lovable runs a security scan">
    Before your app goes live, Lovable automatically runs a basic security scan — checking database configurations, Row Level Security (RLS) rules, cloud project settings, and common misconfiguration patterns. This takes approximately 10–15 seconds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your app is live">
    Once the scan passes, your app is live and accessible at your `*.lovable.app` URL. Share it instantly — no additional steps needed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Your App URL

Every Lovable project gets a unique `*.lovable.app` subdomain the moment you first publish. This URL is permanent, shareable right away, and works on any device. You can find your live URL in the **Share** menu or in your project settings.

If you want your app running under your own domain (like `app.yourcompany.com`), see [Connect a Custom Domain](/publishing/custom-domain).

## Re-Publishing After Changes

Lovable does not push changes to production automatically. Every time you edit your app in the editor, those changes remain in your draft state until you click **Publish** again. This gives you full control over what your users see and when.

<Note>
  Security scanning happens automatically on every publish. If the scan detects a **critical** finding, it can block the publish from completing. Workspace admins can configure this behavior — including enabling auto-fix for non-breaking findings — in the workspace Security Center.
</Note>

## Controlling Who Can See Your App

By default, published apps are publicly accessible to anyone with the URL. You can change this in **Project Settings → Project Visibility**:

- **Public** — anyone with the link can view your app
- **Private** — only workspace members with explicit access can view the app

Use private visibility for internal tools, works-in-progress you're not ready to share, or apps in active development.

## Draft vs. Live: Team Workflows

On **Business** and **Enterprise** plans, editing, approving, and publishing are enforced as separate permissions. This means:

- **Editors** can make changes and iterate freely without affecting production
- **Reviewers** can approve changes before they reach your users
- **Publishers** control when approved changes actually go live

This separation ensures no accidental deploys and gives your team a clear, auditable path from development to production. See [Use Test and Live Environments](/publishing/environments) for more on managing multi-stage workflows.

<Tip>
  Get in the habit of reviewing your changes in the editor preview before publishing — what you see in preview is exactly what your users will see after you hit Publish.
</Tip>

## What Happens to Your Previous Version?

Every time you publish, Lovable saves a record of the deployment. If a new publish introduces an unexpected issue, you can roll back to a previous version from your project history. See [Environments](/publishing/environments) for details on managing Test and Live states safely.
